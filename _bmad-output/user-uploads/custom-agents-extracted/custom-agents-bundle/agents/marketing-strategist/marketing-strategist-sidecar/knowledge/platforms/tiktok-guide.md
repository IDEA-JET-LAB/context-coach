## Platform Overview

### Key Statistics (2024-2025)
- **Monthly Active Users:** 1.5B+ globally
- **Daily Time Spent:** ~95 minutes average
- **Demographics:** 60% Gen Z, growing Millennial adoption
- **Content:** 1B+ videos viewed daily

### Algorithm Fundamentals

TikTok's algorithm prioritizes:

1. **Watch Time** - How long users watch (most important)
2. **Completion Rate** - % who watch to the end
3. **Rewatches** - Looping behavior signals quality
4. **Engagement** - Likes, comments, shares, saves
5. **Profile Visits** - Interest in creator
6. **Follows from Video** - Strong signal

**The FYP (For You Page):**
- Each video gets a "test batch" (~200-500 views)
- Performance determines next batch expansion
- Videos can go viral days/weeks after posting
- Content > follower count (democratic distribution)

## Content Strategy

### Content Pillars for Brands

**1. Educational Content (40%)**
- Tips and how-tos
- Industry insights
- Behind-the-scenes
- Myth-busting

**2. Entertainment (30%)**
- Trends and challenges
- Humor and relatability
- Storytelling
- Satisfying content

**3. Inspirational (20%)**
- Success stories
- Transformations
- Motivational content
- User testimonials

**4. Promotional (10%)**
- Product features
- Offers and launches
- Direct CTAs
- Soft sells integrated into above

### Video Length Strategy

| Length | Best For | Performance |
|--------|----------|-------------|
| **7-15 sec** | Hooks, teasers, trends | High completion, viral potential |
| **15-30 sec** | Tips, quick tutorials | Good engagement, scalable |
| **30-60 sec** | Stories, tutorials | Strong if retention is high |
| **1-3 min** | Deep content, stories | Requires excellent retention |
| **3-10 min** | Long-form (new feature) | Building authority |

### Hook Formulas (First 1-3 Seconds)

**Pattern Interrupt:**
- Unexpected visual or sound
- Movement or action
- Unusual angle or framing

**Curiosity Hooks:**
- "Wait for it..."
- "I can't believe this worked..."
- "Nobody talks about this..."
- "POV: [relatable scenario]"

**Value Promise:**
- "3 things that will [benefit]"
- "How to [outcome] in [time]"
- "The secret to [desirable thing]"

**Controversy/Bold Statement:**
- "Unpopular opinion..."
- "This is why [common thing] is wrong"
- "[Authority] won't tell you this"

## Content Formats

### Trending Formats (Evergreen)

**1. Talking Head**
- Direct to camera
- Works for expertise, opinions, tips
- Add captions always
- Use jump cuts for energy

**2. POV (Point of View)**
- "POV: You're a [role] when [scenario]"
- Relatable situations
- Often humorous
- Strong emotional connection

**3. Tutorial/How-To**
- Step-by-step with visuals
- Text overlay for each step
- Fast-paced editing
- "Follow for part 2" hooks

**4. Duet/Stitch**
- React to other content
- Add commentary or response
- Leverage existing viral content
- Build community

**5. Green Screen**
- Present information
- React to screenshots/articles
- Educational content
- News and commentary

**6. Day in the Life**
- Behind-the-scenes
- Humanize the brand
- Show process/workflow
- Build connection

### Trending Sounds Strategy

**How to Find:**
- Check "Trending" in sound library
- Watch FYP for recurring sounds
- Use Creative Center analytics
- Monitor competitor usage

**When to Use:**
- Early in trend lifecycle (first 1-2 weeks)
- When sound fits brand naturally
- For reach and discovery

**When to Skip:**
- Sound doesn't fit brand
- Trend is overdone (3+ weeks old)
- Original audio serves content better

## Posting Strategy

### Optimal Posting Times

| Day | Best Times (User's Timezone) |
|-----|------------------------------|
| Monday | 6am, 10am, 10pm |
| Tuesday | 2am, 4am, 9am |
| Wednesday | 7am, 8am, 11pm |
| Thursday | 9am, 12pm, 7pm |
| Friday | 5am, 1pm, 3pm |
| Saturday | 11am, 7pm, 8pm |
| Sunday | 7am, 8am, 4pm |

*Note: Test and adjust based on your specific audience analytics.*

### Posting Frequency

| Stage | Frequency | Notes |
|-------|-----------|-------|
| **Testing** | 3-5x/day | Find what works |
| **Growth** | 1-3x/day | Consistent quality |
| **Maintenance** | 1x/day | Sustain momentum |
| **Minimum** | 3-4x/week | Stay in algorithm |

### Hashtag Strategy

**Structure:**
- 3-5 hashtags per video
- Mix of sizes and types

**Hashtag Types:**
1. **Niche** (10K-500K): #saasmarketing, #startuptips
2. **Medium** (500K-5M): #marketingtips, #businessadvice
3. **Broad** (5M+): #fyp, #viral, #learnontiktok

**Best Practices:**
- Don't stuff hashtags
- Place in caption, not comments
- Use relevant hashtags only
- Track which drive views

## TikTok Ads

### Ad Types

**1. In-Feed Ads**
- Appear in FYP
- 9-15 seconds optimal
- Native feel essential
- CTA button available

**2. Spark Ads**
- Boost organic content
- Uses existing posts
- Maintains engagement
- Best for proven content

**3. TopView**
- First ad users see
- Premium placement
- Up to 60 seconds
- Brand awareness focus

**4. Branded Hashtag Challenge**
- User-generated content
- High engagement
- Expensive ($150K+)
- Enterprise level

**5. Branded Effects**
- AR filters and effects
- Interactive engagement
- Brand awareness
- Creative investment required

### Ad Creative Best Practices

**Do:**
- Start with a hook (no logos first)
- Use native TikTok style
- Include captions
- Feature real people
- Use trending sounds (when appropriate)
- Test multiple creatives

**Don't:**
- Use polished TV-style ads
- Start with brand logo
- Use horizontal video
- Ignore sound design
- Use stock footage feel

### Targeting Options

| Targeting Type | Options |
|----------------|---------|
| **Demographics** | Age, gender, location, language |
| **Interests** | Categories and subcategories |
| **Behaviors** | Video interactions, creator interactions |
| **Device** | OS, connection type, device price |
| **Custom** | Customer list, website traffic, app activity |
| **Lookalike** | Based on custom audiences |

### Campaign Structure

```
Campaign Level: Objective
├── Ad Group 1: Broad Targeting (Testing)
│   ├── Creative A
│   ├── Creative B
│   └── Creative C
├── Ad Group 2: Interest Targeting
│   ├── Creative A
│   └── Creative B
└── Ad Group 3: Lookalike
    ├── Creative A
    └── Creative B
```

### Metrics & Benchmarks

## Analytics & Optimization

### Key Metrics to Track

**Video Level:**
- Views and watch time
- Average watch duration
- Traffic sources (FYP vs Following vs Search)
- Audience demographics
- Peak viewing times

**Account Level:**
- Follower growth rate
- Profile views
- Content engagement rate
- Best performing content themes

### Optimization Framework

**Weekly Review:**
1. Top 3 performing videos - why did they work?
2. Bottom 3 videos - what didn't land?
3. Audience insights changes
4. New trends to test

**Monthly Strategy:**
1. Content pillar performance
2. Posting time optimization
3. Format effectiveness
4. Growth trajectory

## Common Mistakes

1. **Posting like other platforms** - TikTok is NOT Instagram or YouTube
2. **Over-produced content** - Authentic > polished
3. **Ignoring trends** - Trends drive discovery
4. **No hooks** - First 1-3 seconds decide everything
5. **Inconsistent posting** - Algorithm rewards consistency
6. **Hashtag stuffing** - Quality > quantity
7. **Ignoring comments** - Engagement drives reach
8. **Not using captions** - 80%+ watch without sound
