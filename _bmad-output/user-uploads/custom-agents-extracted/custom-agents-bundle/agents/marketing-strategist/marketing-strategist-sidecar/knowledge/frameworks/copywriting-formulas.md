# Copywriting Formulas & Templates

> Reference guide for Maven's ad copy and marketing copy creation.

---

## Core Copywriting Principles

### The 4 U's
Every headline should be:
- **Useful** - Benefits the reader
- **Urgent** - Creates time pressure
- **Unique** - Different from competitors
- **Ultra-specific** - Concrete, not vague

### The SLAP Test
- **Stop** - Does it stop the scroll?
- **Look** - Does it make them look?
- **Act** - Does it drive action?
- **Purchase** - Does it lead to purchase?

## Headline Formulas

### Benefit Headlines
- "Get [Benefit] Without [Pain Point]"
- "The [Adjective] Way to [Achieve Outcome]"
- "[Number] Ways to [Benefit] (Even If [Obstacle])"
- "How to [Benefit] in [Timeframe]"
- "Finally, [Solution] That Actually [Works/Delivers]"

### Curiosity Headlines
- "What [Authority] Won't Tell You About [Topic]"
- "The [Hidden/Secret/Surprising] Truth About [Topic]"
- "Why [Conventional Wisdom] Is Wrong"
- "[Number] [Topic] Secrets [Audience] Doesn't Want You to Know"
- "The [Adjective] Mistake That's Costing You [Loss]"

### Social Proof Headlines
- "How [Number] [Audience] [Achieved Result]"
- "Join [Number]+ [Audience] Who [Achievement]"
- "[Authority/Brand] Uses This to [Result]"
- "Rated #1 by [Authority] for [Benefit]"

### Fear/FOMO Headlines
- "Stop [Negative Action] Before It [Consequence]"
- "Are You Making These [Number] [Topic] Mistakes?"
- "[Number] Signs You're [Negative State]"
- "Don't [Action] Until You Read This"
- "Warning: [Problem] Could Be [Consequence]"

### Question Headlines
- "Want to [Benefit] Without [Pain]?"
- "Struggling with [Problem]?"
- "What If You Could [Dream Outcome]?"
- "Ready to [Transform/Achieve/Master] [Topic]?"

## Ad Copy Formulas

### The AIDA Formula
```
[ATTENTION: Hook that stops the scroll]

[INTEREST: Problem statement or relatable situation]

[DESIRE: Benefits, transformation, social proof]

[ACTION: Clear CTA with urgency]
```

**Example:**
```
Tired of posting content that gets 12 likes?

You're spending hours creating content that disappears
into the void. Meanwhile, your competitors are going
viral with less effort.

Our AI-powered content system has helped 2,000+
creators 10x their engagement in 30 days or less.

Start your free trial today. Limited spots available.
[CTA Button: Get Started Free]
```

### The PAS Formula
```
[PROBLEM: State the pain point clearly]

[AGITATION: Intensify the problem, make them feel it]

[SOLUTION: Your answer to the problem]
```

**Example:**
```
Your ads aren't converting.

You've tried everything - new audiences, different
creatives, adjusting budgets. Nothing works. Every
day that passes, you're burning money while your
competitors steal your customers.

[Product] fixes this. Our AI analyzes your campaigns
and tells you exactly what to change. 87% of users
see improved ROAS within 48 hours.
```

### The BAB Formula
```
[BEFORE: Current painful state]

[AFTER: Dream state achieved]

[BRIDGE: How your solution gets them there]
```

**Example:**
```
BEFORE: You're working 60-hour weeks on marketing
that barely moves the needle.

AFTER: Your marketing runs on autopilot, generating
leads while you focus on what you love.

THE BRIDGE: Our marketing automation system sets
up in 30 minutes and works 24/7. Join 5,000+
business owners who got their time back.
```

### The FAB Formula
```
[FEATURE: What it is]
[ADVANTAGE: Why it's better]
[BENEFIT: What it means for them]
```

**Example:**
```
FEATURE: AI-powered content calendar

ADVANTAGE: Creates 30 days of content in 30 minutes
(vs. 10+ hours manually)

BENEFIT: You'll never run out of content ideas again,
and you'll have more time to focus on closing deals.
```

### The 4Ps Formula
```
[PICTURE: Paint a vivid scene of the outcome]

[PROMISE: Make a specific, believable promise]

[PROOF: Evidence that backs up the promise]

[PUSH: Call to action with urgency]
```

## Platform-Specific Copy Templates

### Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram)

**Short-Form (Awareness)**
```
[Hook - 1 line]

[Benefit statement]

[CTA]
```

**Medium-Form (Consideration)**
```
[Hook question or statement]

[Problem acknowledgment]
[Agitation - 1-2 sentences]

[Solution introduction]
[Key benefit 1]
[Key benefit 2]
[Key benefit 3]

[Social proof element]

[CTA with urgency]
```

**Long-Form (Conversion)**
```
[Story hook - personal or customer story]

[Problem expansion - 2-3 paragraphs]

[Solution reveal]

[Benefits list - 3-5 bullets]

[Social proof - testimonial or data]

[Objection handling]

[CTA with urgency + guarantee]
```

### LinkedIn Ads

**Sponsored Content**
```
[Professional hook - industry insight or data]

[Problem statement relevant to B2B]

[Solution positioning]

[Credibility element - customers, results, awards]

[Professional CTA]
```

### TikTok/Reels Ads

**Text Overlay Script**
```
[Hook text - 2-4 words]
↓
[Problem - 3-5 words]
↓
[Solution - 3-5 words]
↓
[Benefit - 3-5 words]
↓
[CTA - 2-3 words]
```

### YouTube Ads

**Bumper (6 sec)**
```
[Visual hook + brand]
[One key message]
[CTA/Logo]
```

**TrueView (15-30 sec)**
```
0-5 sec: Hook + brand mention
5-15 sec: Problem → Solution
15-25 sec: Benefits + proof
25-30 sec: CTA + urgency
```

## Email Copy Templates

### Welcome Email
```
Subject: [Welcome/You're in] + [Benefit tease]

[Personal greeting]

[Acknowledge what they signed up for]

[Set expectations - what they'll receive]

[Quick win or immediate value]

[Next step CTA]

[Sign off]
```

### Promotional Email
```
Subject: [Curiosity or benefit] + [Urgency element]

[Hook - story or attention grabber]

[Problem reminder]

[Solution/offer introduction]

[Benefits - 3 bullet points]

[Social proof]

[Urgency element]

[Primary CTA button]

[FAQ or objection handling]

[Secondary CTA]

P.S. [Restate urgency or bonus]
```

## Persuasion Techniques

### Scarcity
- "Only [X] spots left"
- "Offer ends [date/time]"
- "Limited to first [X] customers"
- "Never offered again at this price"

### Social Proof
- "[Number] customers can't be wrong"
- "Trusted by [brands/people]"
- "★★★★★ [Number] reviews"
- "Featured in [publications]"

### Authority
- "Recommended by [expert]"
- "[Years] of experience"
- "Award-winning"
- "As seen on [platform]"

### Reciprocity
- "Free [valuable thing] included"
- "No credit card required"
- "Free trial, no strings attached"

### Commitment/Consistency
- "You've already taken the first step..."
- "Since you're interested in [topic]..."
- "You're clearly someone who values [value]..."

### Loss Aversion
- "Don't miss out on..."
- "Stop leaving money on the table"
- "Every day without this is costing you..."
