## SaaS Marketing Fundamentals

### The SaaS Marketing Equation

```
Revenue = Traffic × Conversion Rate × ARPU × (1 / Churn Rate)
```

Marketing impacts all four variables:
- **Traffic** - Acquisition channels and campaigns
- **Conversion Rate** - Messaging, targeting, landing pages
- **ARPU** - Upsell marketing, pricing communication
- **Churn** - Retention marketing, customer success content

### SaaS Funnel Stages

```
Awareness → Interest → Consideration → Intent → Evaluation → Purchase → Retention → Advocacy
    ↑          ↑            ↑            ↑          ↑           ↑           ↑          ↑
  Content   Lead Mag    Comparison    Demo/Trial  Social     Pricing    Onboard    Referral
   Ads      Nurture     Content       Request     Proof      Page       Email      Program
```

## Acquisition Channels by Stage

### Pre-PMF (< $100K ARR)

**Focus:** Validation and learning, not scale

| Channel | Priority | Why |
|---------|----------|-----|
| Founder outreach | High | Direct feedback, relationship building |
| Content marketing | Medium | SEO foundation, thought leadership |
| Community building | Medium | Early adopters, feedback loop |
| Paid ads | Low | Only for landing page testing |

### Post-PMF ($100K - $1M ARR)

**Focus:** Finding repeatable acquisition channel

| Channel | Priority | Why |
|---------|----------|-----|
| SEO/Content | High | Scalable, compounds over time |
| Paid search | High | Intent-based, measurable |
| Social organic | Medium | Brand building, engagement |
| Paid social | Medium | Testing audiences, retargeting |
| Partnerships | Medium | Leverage existing audiences |

### Growth Stage ($1M - $10M ARR)

**Focus:** Scaling what works, diversifying

| Channel | Priority | Why |
|---------|----------|-----|
| Performance marketing | High | Scale proven channels |
| Brand marketing | Medium | Long-term demand generation |
| Account-based marketing | Medium | Enterprise expansion |
| Affiliate/Referral | Medium | Efficient growth loops |
| Events/Webinars | Medium | Pipeline generation |

### Scale Stage ($10M+ ARR)

**Focus:** Efficiency and brand dominance

| Channel | Priority | Why |
|---------|----------|-----|
| Multi-channel campaigns | High | Integrated approach |
| Category creation | High | Own the narrative |
| Partner ecosystem | High | Distribution leverage |
| Analyst relations | Medium | Enterprise credibility |
| PR/Media | Medium | Brand authority |

## Trial/Freemium Optimization

### Free Trial Best Practices

**Duration:**
- 7 days: High urgency, quick value products
- 14 days: Standard SaaS, requires setup
- 30 days: Complex products, enterprise

**Type:**
- Credit card required: Higher intent, lower volume
- No credit card: Higher volume, lower intent
- Reverse trial (free then paid features): PLG-optimized

### Trial Conversion Levers

1. **Time-to-Value** - How fast users reach "aha" moment
2. **Activation Sequence** - Onboarding steps that drive conversion
3. **Feature Exposure** - Ensuring trial users see best features
4. **Social Proof** - Testimonials during trial
5. **Urgency** - Trial expiration communication

### Freemium Conversion Strategies

| Strategy | Description | When to Use |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| Feature gating | Lock advanced features | Clear free/paid distinction |
| Usage limits | Cap usage, invite upgrades | Consumption-based value |
| Support gating | Premium support for paid | Service differentiation |
| Collaboration limits | Free = solo, paid = team | Collaboration products |
| Integration limits | Basic integrations free | Platform/API products |

## Pricing Page Optimization

### Essential Elements

1. **Clear tiers** - 3-4 options maximum
2. **Recommended tier** - Visual emphasis on target plan
3. **Feature comparison** - Easy scanning
4. **Social proof** - Customer logos, testimonials
5. **FAQ section** - Address objections
6. **CTA clarity** - One clear action per tier

### Pricing Psychology

- **Anchoring:** Show enterprise tier first (highest price)
- **Decoy effect:** Middle tier looks best vs extremes
- **Charm pricing:** $99 vs $100
- **Annual discount:** Show monthly savings for annual

### Testing Priorities

1. Tier naming
2. Price points
3. Feature packaging
4. CTA copy
5. Social proof placement

## SaaS Email Marketing

### Essential Sequences

#### 1. Onboarding Sequence (Trial Users)
```
Day 0: Welcome + quick start
Day 1: Key feature highlight #1
Day 3: Key feature highlight #2
Day 5: Case study/social proof
Day 7: Trial extension or conversion nudge
Day 10: Final push (if 14-day trial)
```

#### 2. Nurture Sequence (Non-Converters)
```
Week 1: Educational content
Week 2: Case study
Week 3: Industry insights
Week 4: Soft re-engagement
Monthly: Newsletter
```

#### 3. Expansion Sequence (Customers)
```
Month 1: Ensure success, gather feedback
Month 2: Feature adoption
Month 3: Upgrade opportunity
Ongoing: Usage-triggered upsells
```

### Email Metrics Benchmarks (SaaS)

| Metric | Good | Great |
|--------|------|-------|
| Open rate | 20-25% | 30%+ |
| Click rate | 2-3% | 5%+ |
| Trial conversion email | 10% | 20%+ |
| Unsubscribe | <0.5% | <0.2% |

## SaaS Paid Advertising

### Meta Ads Strategy

**Audience Strategy:**
1. Lookalikes from paying customers (1%, 3%, 5%)
2. Website retargeting (visited pricing, started trial)
3. Engagement retargeting (video viewers, page engagers)
4. Interest targeting (job titles, software interests)

**Campaign Structure:**
```
Campaign 1: Acquisition (CBO)
├── Ad Set: Lookalike 1%
├── Ad Set: Lookalike 3%
└── Ad Set: Interest Stack

Campaign 2: Retargeting (ABO)
├── Ad Set: Website Visitors (180d)
├── Ad Set: Trial Abandoners
└── Ad Set: Engaged Non-Converters
```

### Google Ads Strategy

**Campaign Types:**
1. **Brand:** Protect brand terms, high intent
2. **Competitor:** Capture competitive searches
3. **Category:** "Best [category] software"
4. **Problem:** "[Problem] solution"

**Budget Allocation:**
- Brand: 10-20%
- High-intent category: 40-50%
- Competitor: 20-30%
- Problem/educational: 10-20%

### LinkedIn Ads Strategy

**Best for:**
- B2B SaaS with $50+ ACV
- Enterprise-focused products
- Content promotion
- Account-based marketing

**Targeting Options:**
- Job titles/functions
- Company size
- Industry
- Skills
- Groups
- Matched audiences (account lists)

## Metrics & Benchmarks

### Key SaaS Metrics

| Metric | Formula | Benchmark |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| CAC | Total Sales + Marketing Cost / New Customers | Varies by ACV |
| LTV | ARPU × Gross Margin / Churn Rate | 3x+ CAC |
| LTV:CAC | LTV / CAC | 3:1 to 5:1 |
| CAC Payback | CAC / (ARPU × Gross Margin) | <12 months |
| Net Revenue Retention | (Starting MRR + Expansion - Churn) / Starting MRR | >100% |
| Logo Churn | Lost Customers / Total Customers | <5% annually |

### Channel-Specific Benchmarks

| Channel | Metric | Good | Great |
|---------|--------|------|-------|
| Paid Search | CPA | <$100 | <$50 |
| Paid Social | CPA | <$150 | <$75 |
| Organic | Signups/1K visitors | 2% | 5%+ |
| Email | Trial conversion | 10% | 20%+ |
| Referral | % of new revenue | 10% | 25%+ |

## Growth Experiments Framework

### Experiment Prioritization (ICE)

**I**mpact × **C**onfidence × **E**ase = Priority Score

| Score | Rating |
|-------|--------|
| 1-3 | Low |
| 4-6 | Medium |
| 7-9 | High |
| 10 | Maximum |

### Common SaaS Experiments

1. **Pricing page layout** (High impact, easy)
2. **CTA copy variations** (Medium impact, easy)
3. **Trial length changes** (High impact, medium ease)
4. **Onboarding flow** (High impact, harder)
5. **Feature gating** (High impact, harder)

### Experiment Documentation Template

```
Hypothesis: If we [change], then [metric] will [improve] because [reason].
Metric: [Primary metric to measure]
Duration: [X weeks or Y visitors]
Audience: [X% of traffic]
Success criteria: [X% improvement with Y% confidence]
```
