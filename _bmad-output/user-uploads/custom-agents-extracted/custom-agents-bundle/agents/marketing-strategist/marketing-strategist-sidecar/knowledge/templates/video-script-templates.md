# Video Script Templates

> Ready-to-use templates for Maven's video script generation.

---

## Template 1: Short-Form Tutorial (15-30 sec)

**Platform:** TikTok, Reels, Shorts

```
HOOK (0-2 sec):
"Here's how to [achieve outcome] in [X] seconds"
[Visual: Jump cut to action/result preview]

STEP 1 (2-8 sec):
"First, [action]"
[Visual: Show the action clearly]
[Text overlay: "Step 1: [Action]"]

STEP 2 (8-14 sec):
"Then, [action]"
[Visual: Show the action]
[Text overlay: "Step 2: [Action]"]

STEP 3 (14-22 sec):
"Finally, [action]"
[Visual: Show the action + result]
[Text overlay: "Step 3: [Action]"]

RESULT (22-28 sec):
"And that's how you [outcome]"
[Visual: Show completed result]

CTA (28-30 sec):
"Follow for more [topic] tips"
[Visual: Point to follow button or profile]
```

## Template 2: Problem-Solution (30-45 sec)

**Platform:** TikTok, Reels, Shorts

```
HOOK (0-3 sec):
"Stop doing [common mistake]"
OR
"If you're struggling with [problem], watch this"
[Visual: Eye contact, confident delivery]

PROBLEM (3-12 sec):
"Most people [wrong approach], and it leads to [negative outcome]"
[Visual: Show or demonstrate the problem]
[Text overlay: Key phrase]

AGITATION (12-20 sec):
"This means you're [consequence], [consequence], and [consequence]"
[Visual: Quick cuts showing frustration/failure]

SOLUTION (20-35 sec):
"Instead, try [solution]"
"Here's why it works: [brief explanation]"
[Visual: Demonstrate the solution]
[Text overlay: Key steps or tip]

CTA (35-45 sec):
"Save this for later and follow for more [topic]"
[Visual: Point to save/follow]
```

## Template 3: Story-Based (45-90 sec)

**Platform:** TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts

```
HOOK (0-3 sec):
"[Outcome achieved] by doing one thing differently"
OR
"What I learned after [significant experience]"
[Visual: Intense eye contact or B-roll of outcome]

SETUP (3-15 sec):
"[Time period] ago, I was [situation]"
"I was [struggling with / trying to] [challenge]"
[Visual: B-roll or reenactment of past state]
[Text: Context or timeline]

CONFLICT (15-35 sec):
"I tried [approach 1], but [it didn't work because]"
"Then I tried [approach 2], same problem"
"I was about to [give up / try something desperate]"
[Visual: Show frustration, attempts, failure]

TURNING POINT (35-55 sec):
"Then I realized [insight]"
"Instead of [old approach], I [new approach]"
[Visual: Moment of clarity, switch to success B-roll]

RESULT (55-70 sec):
"Within [timeframe], I [achieved outcome]"
"Now I [current better state]"
[Visual: Show proof, results, transformation]

LESSON + CTA (70-90 sec):
"The lesson: [one-sentence takeaway]"
"If you're dealing with [similar problem], try [advice]"
"Follow for more [topic] content"
[Visual: Direct to camera, confident]
```

## Template 5: YouTube Long-Form Tutorial (5-15 min)

**Platform:** YouTube

```
HOOK (0-15 sec):
"In this video, I'm going to show you exactly how to [outcome], even if you [objection/obstacle]"
"By the end, you'll be able to [specific result]"
[Visual: Quick montage of result or process]

INTRO (15-45 sec):
"Hey, I'm [Name], and I help [audience] [achieve outcome]"
"If that sounds like you, hit subscribe and let's dive in"
[Visual: Face to camera, energy]

WHY THIS MATTERS (45 sec - 1:30):
"Before we get into the how, let's talk about why this matters"
"[Problem statement and consequences]"
"[Why solving this changes things]"
[Visual: B-roll, graphics, or face to camera]

OVERVIEW (1:30 - 2:00):
"Here's what we're going to cover:"
"First, [topic 1]"
"Then, [topic 2]"
"And finally, [topic 3]"
[Visual: List on screen or graphics]

SECTION 1 (2:00 - 5:00):
"Let's start with [topic 1]"
"[Teaching content with examples]"
"[Demonstration or walkthrough]"
"Key takeaway: [summary]"
[Visual: Screen share, demos, B-roll as needed]

SECTION 2 (5:00 - 9:00):
"Now let's move on to [topic 2]"
"[Teaching content]"
"[Examples and demonstrations]"
[Visual: Vary between talking head and demos]

SECTION 3 (9:00 - 12:00):
"Finally, [topic 3]"
"This is where most people go wrong"
"[Teaching content]"
[Visual: Emphasize key points with graphics]

RECAP (12:00 - 13:00):
"So to recap:"
"[Key point 1]"
"[Key point 2]"
"[Key point 3]"
[Visual: Summary graphic or text]

CTA + CLOSE (13:00 - 14:00):
"If you found this helpful, hit like and subscribe"
"And if you want to go deeper, [next video / resource]"
"I'll see you in the next one"
[Visual: End screen with recommended video]
```

## Customization Variables

When using these templates, replace bracketed items:

| Variable | Replace With |
|----------|--------------|
| `[outcome]` | Specific result (e.g., "10x your followers") |
| `[topic]` | Your subject area |
| `[audience]` | Target viewer (e.g., "marketers") |
| `[timeframe]` | Specific time (e.g., "30 days") |
| `[problem]` | Pain point |
| `[solution]` | Your method/product |
| `[number]` | Specific count (odd numbers often perform better) |
| `[Name]` | Creator/brand name |
| `[Product]` | Product/service name |

---

*Maven uses these templates as starting points, customizing for brand voice and platform.*
