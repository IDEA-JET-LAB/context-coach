## Algorithm Fundamentals

### How LinkedIn Ranks Content

**1. Immediate Signals (First Hour)**
- Engagement velocity (likes, comments)
- Dwell time (how long people stop to read)
- Content quality signals

**2. Content Classification**
- Spam → Killed
- Low Quality → Limited distribution
- Clear → Shown to connections, evaluated
- High Quality → Expanded distribution

**3. Network Reach Expansion**
- 1st degree connections (initial test)
- 2nd degree (if performing well)
- 3rd degree and beyond (viral potential)

### What Gets Reach

**Do:**
- Original, text-based content
- Native video and documents
- Meaningful comments and discussions
- Personal stories and insights
- Industry expertise and opinions
- Engagement with others' content

**Don't:**
- External links in posts (massive reach penalty)
- Engagement pods (detected and penalized)
- Follow/unfollow tactics
- Automation that violates ToS
- Purely promotional content

## Content Strategy

### Content Types Performance

| Format | Reach | Engagement | Best For |
|--------|-------|------------|----------|
| **Text Post** | High | High | Stories, insights, opinions |
| **Document/Carousel** | High | Very High | Educational content, guides |
| **Native Video** | Medium | High | Personal brand, demonstrations |
| **Image + Text** | Medium | Medium | Visual stories, infographics |
| **Poll** | High | High | Engagement, data gathering |
| **Article** | Low | Medium | Long-form, SEO value |
| **Link Post** | Low | Low | Traffic (but limited reach) |
| **Newsletter** | Medium | High | Subscriber relationship |

### Content Pillars for B2B

**1. Expertise (40%)**
- Industry insights and analysis
- How-to content and tutorials
- Data and research sharing
- Tool and resource recommendations

**2. Story (30%)**
- Personal experiences and lessons
- Behind-the-scenes
- Career journey moments
- Failures and learnings

**3. Opinion (20%)**
- Hot takes on industry trends
- Contrarian viewpoints
- Future predictions
- Commentary on news

**4. Promotional (10%)**
- Product/service mentions
- Company updates
- Case studies and results
- Job openings and hiring

### Post Structure (Text)

**The Viral Post Structure:**
```
[Hook Line - Stop the scroll]

[Space - Creates curiosity]

[Body - Short paragraphs, easy to read]
- Use line breaks often
- Keep lines short
- Use bullets when listing
- Include a story or insight

[Space]

[Call to Engagement or CTA]
```

**Example:**
```
I lost a $50,000 deal because of one email.

And I'd do it again.

Here's why:

The prospect asked us to lower our price by 40%.

Instead of negotiating, I said:

"I appreciate you considering us, but we can't
deliver the results you need at that price point.

I'd rather lose the deal than under-deliver."

Two weeks later, they came back.

Full price.

Because they realized: if we won't compromise
on price, we won't compromise on quality.

Sometimes the best deals are the ones you
walk away from.

What's a deal you're glad you didn't take?
```

## Personal Brand Strategy

### Why Personal > Company

- Personal posts get 5-10x more reach than company pages
- People trust people, not logos
- Algorithm favors personal accounts
- Easier to build authentic connections

### Building Your Personal Brand

**1. Define Your Niche**
- What topics will you own?
- Who is your target audience?
- What unique perspective do you bring?

**2. Content Consistency**
- Post 3-5x per week minimum
- Same themes and topics
- Recognizable voice and style

**3. Engagement Strategy**
- Comment meaningfully on others' posts
- Respond to all comments on your posts
- Build relationships, not just reach

**4. Profile Optimization**
- Headline: What you do + who you help
- About: Story + value + CTA
- Featured: Best content and resources
- Experience: Achievements, not just roles

### Headline Formulas

**The Value Statement:**
"I help [audience] achieve [outcome] through [method]"

**The Authority Position:**
"[Role] | [Expertise area] | [Achievement or credential]"

**The Conversation Starter:**
"Talking about [topic] | [Role] at [Company]"

## LinkedIn Ads

### Ad Formats

| Format | Best For | Cost |
|--------|----------|------|
| **Sponsored Content** | Awareness, leads | $$$ |
| **Message Ads (InMail)** | Direct outreach | $$$$ |
| **Text Ads** | Traffic, awareness | $ |
| **Dynamic Ads** | Personalized targeting | $$ |
| **Video Ads** | Brand awareness | $$$ |
| **Carousel Ads** | Multiple products/features | $$$ |
| **Conversation Ads** | Conversational lead gen | $$$ |
| **Lead Gen Forms** | Frictionless leads | $$$ |

### Targeting Options

**Professional Targeting (LinkedIn's Strength):**
- Job title and function
- Seniority level
- Company name and size
- Industry
- Skills
- Groups
- Education

**Audience Expansion:**
- Matched audiences (email lists)
- Website retargeting
- Lookalike audiences
- Account lists (ABM)

### Campaign Structure

**For Lead Generation:**
```
Campaign 1: Cold Audience (CBO)
├── Ad Set: Job Titles (Target roles)
│   ├── Ad: Pain point focused
│   └── Ad: Solution focused
├── Ad Set: Industry (Vertical specific)
│   ├── Ad: Industry-specific copy
│   └── Ad: Case study reference
└── Ad Set: Competitor (Matched audience)
    └── Ad: Differentiation focused

Campaign 2: Retargeting (ABO)
├── Ad Set: Website Visitors
├── Ad Set: Engaged (Video viewers, page visitors)
└── Ad Set: Lead Nurture
```

### Lead Gen Forms Best Practices

**Form Fields:**
- Pre-filled from profile (frictionless)
- Ask only what you need
- 3-4 fields optimal
- Include privacy policy

**Offer Types:**
- eBooks and guides
- Webinar registration
- Demo requests
- Free tools/templates

### Ad Benchmarks

| Metric | Average | Good |
|--------|---------|------|
| CTR (Sponsored Content) | 0.4-0.6% | >0.8% |
| CTR (Text Ads) | 0.02-0.05% | >0.08% |
| Cost per Click | $5-10 | <$5 |
| Cost per Lead | $50-100 | <$50 |
| InMail Open Rate | 50% | >60% |
| InMail CTR | 3-4% | >5% |

## Analytics & Optimization

### Key Metrics - Organic

**Post Level:**
- Impressions
- Engagement rate
- Comments (most valuable)
- Shares (reach expansion)
- Profile visits (interest signal)

**Profile Level:**
- Search appearances
- Post views
- Profile views
- Follower growth

### Engagement Rate Calculation

```
Engagement Rate = (Likes + Comments + Shares) / Impressions × 100
```

**Benchmarks:**
- Below average: <2%
- Average: 2-5%
- Good: 5-10%
- Excellent: >10%

### Performance Review Process

**Daily:**
- Respond to comments
- Engage with network
- Monitor post performance

**Weekly:**
- Analyze top posts
- Identify patterns
- Adjust content mix

**Monthly:**
- Review follower growth
- Analyze audience demographics
- Plan content calendar

## B2B Lead Generation Tactics

### Organic Lead Gen

**1. Content-to-DM Funnel**
- Post valuable content
- Include "DM me for [resource]"
- Qualify and nurture in messages

**2. Comment Strategy**
- Comment on target accounts' posts
- Provide genuine value
- Build relationship before pitch

**3. Connection Strategy**
- Send personalized connection requests
- Reference shared interests or content
- Follow up with value, not pitch

### ABM on LinkedIn

**Account-Based Strategy:**
1. Define target account list
2. Upload to Matched Audiences
3. Target decision-makers
4. Multi-touchpoint campaigns
5. Coordinate with sales outreach

## Common Mistakes

1. **Treating like Facebook** - LinkedIn is professional, not personal life
2. **Only posting company content** - Personal posts get 10x reach
3. **Ignoring comments** - Replies boost reach and build relationships
4. **External links in posts** - Put in comments or bio
5. **Inconsistent posting** - Algorithm rewards consistency
6. **Sales pitches in connection requests** - Build relationship first
7. **Ignoring engagement pods** - They're detectable and penalized
8. **Not using documents/carousels** - Highest engagement format
9. **Generic content** - Be specific to your niche
10. **No personal stories** - Stories humanize and connect
