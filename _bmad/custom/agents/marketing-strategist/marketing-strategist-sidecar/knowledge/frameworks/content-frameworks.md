# Content Creation Frameworks

> Reference guide for Maven's content creation recommendations.

---

## Hook Formulas

### The Curiosity Hook
**Pattern:** "Most people don't know that [surprising fact]..."
**Works because:** Creates information gap the viewer wants to close.
**Best for:** Educational content, myth-busting, industry insights.

### The Controversy Hook
**Pattern:** "Unpopular opinion: [bold statement]"
**Works because:** Triggers emotional response and engagement.
**Best for:** Thought leadership, starting conversations, viral potential.
**Caution:** Must be authentic and defensible.

### The Value Promise Hook
**Pattern:** "Here's how to [achieve desired outcome] in [timeframe]"
**Works because:** Clear benefit with specificity builds trust.
**Best for:** How-to content, tutorials, actionable advice.

### The Story Hook
**Pattern:** "Last week, [something happened] and it changed everything..."
**Works because:** Humans are wired for narrative.
**Best for:** Case studies, lessons learned, personal brand content.

### The Question Hook
**Pattern:** "What if I told you [counterintuitive idea]?"
**Works because:** Engages the brain to seek an answer.
**Best for:** Challenging assumptions, introducing new concepts.

### The Number Hook
**Pattern:** "[X] things that [specific audience] gets wrong about [topic]"
**Works because:** Specific numbers create expectation and scannability.
**Best for:** List content, tips, mistakes to avoid.

### The Challenge Hook
**Pattern:** "I bet you can't [complete this challenge]"
**Works because:** Competitive instinct drives engagement.
**Best for:** Interactive content, UGC campaigns, viral mechanics.

## Content Structures

### The AIDA Framework
**Attention** → Grab attention with a hook
**Interest** → Build interest with relevant information
**Desire** → Create desire with benefits and transformation
**Action** → Clear call-to-action

**Best for:** Sales content, landing pages, ad copy.

### The PAS Framework
**Problem** → Identify the pain point
**Agitation** → Intensify the problem (make them feel it)
**Solution** → Present your solution

**Best for:** Problem-aware audiences, pain-point marketing.

### The BAB Framework
**Before** → Current state (with problems)
**After** → Desired state (problems solved)
**Bridge** → How to get there (your solution)

**Best for:** Transformation content, testimonials, case studies.

### The 4Ps Framework
**Picture** → Paint a vivid scene
**Promise** → Make a specific promise
**Proof** → Provide evidence
**Push** → Call to action

**Best for:** Persuasive content, sales pages.

### The PASTOR Framework
**Problem** → Identify the problem
**Amplify** → Amplify consequences
**Story** → Tell a relatable story
**Transformation** → Show the transformation
**Offer** → Present the offer
**Response** → Request action

**Best for:** Long-form sales content, webinars.

## Video Content Structures

### The Tutorial Structure
1. **Hook** (0-3 sec): State the outcome
2. **Credibility** (3-10 sec): Why listen to you
3. **Steps** (main content): Numbered, clear steps
4. **Recap** (near end): Quick summary
5. **CTA** (end): What to do next

### The Story Structure
1. **Hook** (0-3 sec): Tease the ending
2. **Setup** (3-15 sec): Context and characters
3. **Conflict** (middle): The challenge/problem
4. **Resolution** (near end): How it was solved
5. **Lesson** (end): Takeaway + CTA

### The Listicle Structure
1. **Hook** (0-3 sec): "[X] things that will [benefit]"
2. **Tease** (3-5 sec): "Number [X] changed everything"
3. **List Items**: One at a time with visuals
4. **Best One**: Save best for last
5. **CTA**: Engage (comment your favorite)

### The Comparison Structure
1. **Hook**: "Which is better: [A] vs [B]?"
2. **Context**: Why this matters
3. **A Breakdown**: Pros/cons
4. **B Breakdown**: Pros/cons
5. **Verdict**: Your recommendation + CTA

## Content Pillar Framework

### The 3-3-3 Model
**3 Core Pillars** - Main topics you own
**3 Content Types** - Formats you create
**3 Audience Segments** - Who you serve

**Example:**
- Pillars: Marketing Strategy, AI Tools, SaaS Growth
- Types: Short video, Long video, Written posts
- Segments: Founders, Marketers, Creators

### Content Pillar Selection Criteria
1. **Expertise** - You have real knowledge/experience
2. **Audience Interest** - They actually want this
3. **Differentiation** - Your unique angle
4. **Scalability** - Can create consistently
5. **Business Alignment** - Supports your goals

## Engagement Frameworks

### The 5-3-1 Engagement Rule
**5 value posts** → Educational, entertaining, inspirational
**3 engagement posts** → Questions, polls, discussions
**1 promotional post** → Direct CTA, offer, pitch

### The Conversation Starter Framework
1. **Make a claim** (not a question)
2. **Share your reasoning**
3. **Invite disagreement** ("But I might be wrong...")
4. **Ask for their take**

### The Shareability Framework
Content gets shared when it makes the sharer look:
- **Smart** (insights, data, analysis)
- **Funny** (humor, relatability)
- **Caring** (causes, values)
- **Helpful** (resources, tips)
- **First** (breaking news, trends)

## Platform-Specific Structures

### TikTok Viral Structure
1. **Pattern interrupt** (0-1 sec): Visual/audio shock
2. **Hook** (1-3 sec): Curiosity or promise
3. **Body** (3-45 sec): Deliver value with visual changes every 3-5 sec
4. **Loop setup** (end): Creates reason to rewatch

### YouTube Short-Form Structure
1. **Thumbnail moment** (0-2 sec): Most visual moment first
2. **Hook** (2-5 sec): Clear value promise
3. **Delivery** (5-50 sec): Quick, valuable content
4. **Subscribe CTA** (end): Simple ask

### LinkedIn Post Structure
1. **Hook line** (line 1): Stop the scroll
2. **Space** (line break): Creates curiosity
3. **Body** (5-10 lines): Value delivery, short lines
4. **Space**
5. **CTA or question**: Drive engagement

### Instagram Carousel Structure
1. **Slide 1**: Hook + topic (curiosity gap)
2. **Slides 2-8**: One point per slide, visual consistency
3. **Slide 9**: Summary or key takeaway
4. **Slide 10**: CTA (save, share, follow)

## CTA Frameworks

### The Soft CTA
"If you found this helpful, [low-commitment action]"
- Follow for more
- Save this for later
- Share with someone who needs this

### The Value CTA
"Want more? [offer additional value]"
- Grab the free guide in bio
- Comment [word] for the full template
- DM me [word] for the checklist

### The Engagement CTA
"I want to hear from you: [question]"
- What's your biggest challenge with X?
- Which tip was most helpful?
- Drop your hot take below

### The Direct CTA
"Ready to [outcome]? [specific action]"
- Book a call: [link]
- Get started: [link]
- Join the waitlist: [link]
