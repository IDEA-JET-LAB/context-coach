## Algorithm Fundamentals

### How X Ranks Content

**Primary Signals:**
1. **Engagement velocity** - Likes, replies, reposts in first hour
2. **Author reputation** - Follower count, engagement history
3. **Relevance** - Topic match to user interests
4. **Recency** - Newer content prioritized
5. **Media type** - Native content favored

**X Premium Boost:**
- Premium subscribers get algorithmic priority
- Replies appear higher in threads
- Longer content allowed
- Edit functionality
- Revenue sharing eligibility

### Feed Tabs

| Tab | Content |
|-----|---------|
| **For You** | Algorithmic feed (default) |
| **Following** | Chronological from follows |
| **Lists** | Curated account groups |

### What Gets Reach

**Do:**
- Native text posts (no links)
- Threads with valuable content
- Engagement bait that's authentic
- Real-time commentary on events
- Strong opinions and hot takes
- Replies on large accounts

**Don't:**
- External links in main post (put in reply)
- Excessive hashtags
- Engagement pods
- Mass automation
- Repetitive content

## Content Strategy

### Content Types Performance

| Format | Reach | Engagement | Best For |
|--------|-------|------------|----------|
| **Text Post** | High | High | Opinions, insights, updates |
| **Thread** | Very High | Very High | Education, stories, deep dives |
| **Image + Text** | Medium-High | High | Visual content, infographics |
| **Video** | Medium | High | Tutorials, behind-scenes |
| **Poll** | High | Very High | Engagement, research |
| **Link Post** | Low | Medium | Traffic (but limited reach) |
| **Spaces** | Medium | Very High | Real-time conversation |

### Content Pillars for X

**The 4-Pillar Approach:**

1. **Expertise (40%)**
   - Industry insights and analysis
   - Tips and how-tos
   - Data and research
   - Tool recommendations

2. **Opinion (30%)**
   - Hot takes on trends
   - Contrarian viewpoints
   - Commentary on news
   - Predictions

3. **Story (20%)**
   - Personal experiences
   - Lessons learned
   - Behind-the-scenes
   - Wins and failures

4. **Engagement (10%)**
   - Questions to audience
   - Polls
   - Reposting community content
   - Replying to followers

### Post Formats

**The Single Tweet:**
```
[Hook - First line that stops the scroll]

[Body - Supporting content, insight, or story]

[CTA or Engagement prompt - optional]
```

**Example:**
```
Most founders get marketing completely wrong.

They spend months on "brand awareness" before
they have a single paying customer.

Here's the truth: revenue is the best marketing.

Get 10 customers who love you first.
Everything else becomes easier.
```

**The Question Post:**
```
[Observation or setup]

[Question to drive replies]
```

**Example:**
```
Every successful founder I know has one thing in common.

What's the #1 trait you've noticed in successful founders?
```

## Thread Strategy

### Why Threads Work
- Multiple hooks to capture attention
- Higher dwell time signals quality
- Each tweet can be shared independently
- Shows depth of expertise
- Bookmark-worthy content

### Thread Structure

**The Hook Tweet (1):**
```
[Bold claim or promise]

[Why reader should care]

🧵 Thread:
```

**The Body Tweets (2-9):**
```
[Point 1 with explanation]

[Point 2 with example]

[Point 3 with proof]

... continue pattern
```

**The Closer Tweet (10):**
```
[Summary or key takeaway]

[CTA - Follow for more / Like & repost if helpful]

[Link to resource - optional]
```

### Thread Best Practices

**Do:**
- Number your tweets (1/, 2/, etc.)
- Make each tweet standalone valuable
- Use line breaks for readability
- Include visuals where helpful
- End with clear CTA
- Tease thread in first tweet

**Don't:**
- Make threads too long (5-12 tweets ideal)
- Pad with filler content
- Forget to connect tweets logically
- Skip the CTA
- Bury the best content at the end

### Thread Templates

**The Listicle Thread:**
```
Tweet 1: [X] [things] that will [benefit]:

Tweet 2: 1. [Thing 1]
[Explanation]

Tweet 3: 2. [Thing 2]
[Explanation]

... continue

Final Tweet: Summary + CTA
```

**The Story Thread:**
```
Tweet 1: [Outcome achieved]. Here's how:

Tweet 2: [Setup/Background]

Tweet 3: [The challenge/problem]

Tweet 4: [Failed attempt 1]

Tweet 5: [Failed attempt 2]

Tweet 6: [The breakthrough]

Tweet 7: [The result]

Tweet 8: [Key lesson]

Final Tweet: [CTA]
```

**The How-To Thread:**
```
Tweet 1: How to [achieve outcome] (step-by-step):

Tweet 2: Step 1: [Action]
[Details]

Tweet 3: Step 2: [Action]
[Details]

... continue

Final Tweet: Summary + CTA
```

## Engagement Strategy

### The Reply Strategy

**Why Replies Matter:**
- Appear in notifications of large accounts
- Get seen by their followers
- Build relationships with influencers
- Algorithm values reply engagement

**How to Reply Effectively:**
1. Add genuine value (not just "great post!")
2. Share a relevant experience
3. Ask a thoughtful follow-up question
4. Offer a different perspective
5. Be early (first hour matters most)

### The Engagement Ladder

```
Level 1: Consume content (lurk)
    ↓
Level 2: Like posts (passive engagement)
    ↓
Level 3: Reply to posts (active engagement)
    ↓
Level 4: Repost with comment (amplification)
    ↓
Level 5: Create original content (leadership)
```

### Building Your Network

**Follow Strategy:**
- Follow people in your niche
- Follow your target audience
- Follow complementary accounts
- Engage before following

**Getting Follows:**
- Consistent valuable content
- Active replies on larger accounts
- Threads that get shared
- Cross-promotion from other platforms

## X Spaces

### What Are Spaces?
- Live audio conversations
- Host or co-host discussions
- Listeners can request to speak
- Can be scheduled or impromptu
- Recordings available (Premium)

### Spaces Best Practices

**Hosting:**
- Pick focused topic
- Promote 24-48 hours ahead
- Have 2-3 co-hosts
- Prepare talking points
- Set clear format (interview, panel, Q&A)
- Record and repurpose

**Growing with Spaces:**
- Join others' Spaces first
- Request to speak and add value
- Co-host with larger accounts
- Cross-promote to email list
- Clip and share highlights

## Hashtag Strategy

### Hashtag Best Practices

**Current Reality:**
- Hashtags less important than before
- 1-2 relevant hashtags maximum
- Don't use hashtags mid-sentence
- Place at end if using

**When to Use:**
- Event participation (#conference)
- Campaign tracking (#brandcampaign)
- Trending topics (if genuinely relevant)
- Community tags (#buildinpublic)

**When to Skip:**
- Most regular posts
- When it looks spammy
- Unrelated trending tags

## Analytics & Optimization

### Key Metrics

**Post Level:**
- Impressions
- Engagements (likes, replies, reposts, clicks)
- Engagement rate
- Profile clicks
- Link clicks

**Account Level:**
- Follower growth
- Profile visits
- Mention volume
- Top tweets

### Engagement Rate Calculation

```
Engagement Rate = (Likes + Replies + Reposts + Clicks) / Impressions × 100
```

**Benchmarks:**
- Below average: <1%
- Average: 1-3%
- Good: 3-6%
- Excellent: >6%

### Best Posting Times

| Day | Best Times (US) |
|-----|-----------------|
| Monday-Friday | 8-10am, 12-1pm |
| Weekend | 9-11am |

*Note: Test for your specific audience using analytics.*

### Performance Review

**Daily:**
- Respond to replies and mentions
- Engage with network
- Monitor post performance

**Weekly:**
- Analyze top performing tweets
- Identify patterns
- Plan next week's content

**Monthly:**
- Review follower growth
- Analyze audience demographics
- Adjust content strategy

## Growth Tactics

### The Compound Growth Strategy

**Week 1-4: Foundation**
- Post 1-2x daily
- Reply to 10+ accounts daily
- Build initial following

**Week 5-8: Content**
- Publish 1-2 threads per week
- Test different content angles
- Double down on what works

**Week 9-12: Scale**
- Increase posting frequency
- Launch Spaces or collaborations
- Cross-promote from other platforms

### Quick Wins

1. **Optimize profile** - Clear bio, professional photo
2. **Pin best tweet** - Your most impressive content
3. **Reply strategy** - Comment on larger accounts
4. **Thread consistently** - 1-2 per week minimum
5. **Engage genuinely** - Build real relationships

### Collaboration Tactics

- Co-host Spaces with complementary accounts
- Mutual quote tweets
- Thread collaborations
- Shoutout exchanges
- Community building

## Common Mistakes

1. **Only posting links** - Links get killed by algorithm
2. **Not engaging** - X rewards active users
3. **Inconsistent posting** - Breaks momentum
4. **Too promotional** - Value first, sell later
5. **Ignoring replies** - Missing relationship building
6. **Generic content** - Be specific and opinionated
7. **Hashtag stuffing** - Looks spammy
8. **Buying followers** - Kills engagement rate
9. **Not using threads** - Missing high-performing format
10. **Copying without adapting** - Find your voice
