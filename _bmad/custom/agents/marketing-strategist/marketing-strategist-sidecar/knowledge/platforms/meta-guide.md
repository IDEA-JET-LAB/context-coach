## Algorithm Fundamentals

### Facebook Algorithm

**Ranking Signals:**
1. **Relationship** - Interaction history with poster
2. **Content Type** - User's preferred formats
3. **Recency** - Newer content prioritized
4. **Engagement Probability** - Predicted interaction

**What Gets Reach:**
- Content from friends/family (personal)
- Meaningful interactions (comments > likes)
- Native video (especially longer watch time)
- Group content (community engagement)

**What Gets Penalized:**
- Engagement bait ("Like if you agree!")
- Clickbait headlines
- External links (reduced reach)
- Misinformation/policy violations

### Instagram Algorithm

**Feed & Explore:**
1. **Interest** - Predicted engagement based on history
2. **Relationship** - Interaction frequency
3. **Timeliness** - Recency matters
4. **Session Time** - Varies by user behavior

**Reels Algorithm:**
1. **Watch time & completion** - Most important
2. **Engagement** - Likes, comments, shares, saves
3. **Audio usage** - Trending sounds boost reach
4. **Content signals** - Visual quality, interest match

**Stories:**
- Chronological with engagement weighting
- Interaction prompts boost reach
- Consistency keeps you at front

## Content Strategy

### Facebook Content Types

| Format | Best For | Engagement |
|--------|----------|------------|
| **Native Video** | Engagement, reach | High |
| **Live Video** | Real-time engagement | Very high |
| **Photo/Carousel** | Storytelling, products | Medium |
| **Text Post** | Questions, discussions | Medium |
| **Link Posts** | Traffic driving | Lower reach |
| **Stories** | Daily engagement | Medium |
| **Reels** | Discovery, reach | Growing |

### Instagram Content Types

| Format | Best For | Engagement |
|--------|----------|------------|
| **Reels** | Discovery, viral reach | Highest |
| **Stories** | Daily touchpoints | High |
| **Carousel Posts** | Education, saves | High |
| **Single Image** | Brand moments | Medium |
| **IGTV/Long Video** | Deep content | Lower |
| **Live** | Real-time connection | High |

### Content Pillars (Instagram)

**The 3 E's Framework:**
1. **Educate (40%)** - Tips, how-tos, insights
2. **Entertain (30%)** - Trends, humor, relatable
3. **Engage (20%)** - Questions, polls, UGC
4. **Promote (10%)** - Products, offers, CTAs

### Reels Strategy

**Best Practices:**
- Hook in first 0.5-1 second
- Use trending audio
- Text overlays for accessibility
- 15-30 seconds optimal
- Clear CTA or loop point

**Content Ideas:**
- Behind-the-scenes
- Tutorials and tips
- Trend participation
- Before/after
- Day in the life
- Relatable moments

## Facebook Groups Strategy

### Why Groups Work
- Higher organic reach than Pages
- Direct community engagement
- Member-to-member interaction
- Trust and loyalty building
- Email-like direct access

### Group Types

1. **Support/Help** - Answer questions, provide value
2. **Interest-Based** - Around topic, not brand
3. **Customer Community** - For existing customers
4. **Learning/Course** - Educational programs
5. **Local/Regional** - Geographic communities

### Growing a Group

1. **Clear purpose** - Specific value proposition
2. **Quality > quantity** - Engaged members matter
3. **Regular content** - Daily posts and prompts
4. **Member spotlights** - Feature active members
5. **Exclusive value** - Content only in group
6. **Cross-promote** - From Page, email, other channels

### Audience Strategy

**Core Audiences:**
| Targeting | Best For |
|-----------|----------|
| Demographics | Broad reach |
| Interests | Topic relevance |
| Behaviors | Action-based |
| Connections | Page/app engagement |

**Custom Audiences:**
| Source | Use Case |
|--------|----------|
| Customer List | Existing customers |
| Website Traffic | Retargeting |
| App Activity | User engagement |
| Engagement | Social interactions |
| Video Views | Interest qualification |

**Lookalike Audiences:**
- 1% = Most similar (highest quality)
- 1-5% = Balanced reach/quality
- 5-10% = Broader reach

### Campaign Structure

**Testing Phase:**
```
Campaign (CBO)
├── Ad Set 1: Lookalike 1% Customers
│   ├── Ad 1: Video creative
│   ├── Ad 2: Image creative
│   └── Ad 3: Carousel creative
├── Ad Set 2: Lookalike 3% Customers
│   ├── Ad 1: Video creative
│   └── Ad 2: Image creative
└── Ad Set 3: Interest Stack
    ├── Ad 1: Video creative
    └── Ad 2: Image creative
```

**Scaling Phase:**
```
Campaign 1: Winning Audiences (CBO, 70% budget)
├── Proven Ad Set 1
├── Proven Ad Set 2
└── Proven Ad Set 3

Campaign 2: Testing (ABO, 30% budget)
├── New Audience Test
├── New Creative Test
└── New Angle Test
```

### Creative Best Practices

**Image Ads:**
- Minimal text (check text overlay tool)
- High contrast, eye-catching
- Product clearly visible
- Mobile-optimized (test on phone)

**Video Ads:**
- Hook in first 3 seconds
- Design for sound-off (captions)
- Square or vertical for mobile
- 15-30 seconds for engagement
- Clear CTA

**Carousel Ads:**
- Tell a story across cards
- Best products first (and last)
- Consistent visual style
- Mix of lifestyle and product

**Copy Best Practices:**
- Lead with benefit or hook
- Short primary text (125 chars visible)
- Use emojis sparingly
- Clear CTA
- Test long vs short copy

## Common Mistakes

1. **Posting and ghosting** - Engage with comments
2. **Only promotional content** - Follow 80/20 rule
3. **Ignoring Reels** - Primary discovery format
4. **Poor-quality visuals** - Instagram is visual-first
5. **Inconsistent posting** - Algorithm rewards consistency
6. **Not using Stories** - Daily touchpoints matter
7. **Broad ad targeting** - Start specific, expand
8. **One creative in ads** - Always test multiples
9. **Ignoring comments/DMs** - Engagement drives reach
10. **External links in posts** - Use bio link or Stories
